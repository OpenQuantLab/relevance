Fit measures how well the relevant observations explain this particular prediction. It is available before the outcome is known.

Method

  1. Fetch daily prices and build a monthly panel of momentum and volatility features.
  2. For each month, compute the relevance of every past observation to the current circumstances (informativeness plus similarity, in Mahalanobis space).
  3. Predict the forward return as the relevance-weighted average of past outcomes, keeping only the most relevant subset.
  4. Rank the predictions, form quintile portfolios, and measure IC, spread, Sharpe ratio, drawdown and turnover.
